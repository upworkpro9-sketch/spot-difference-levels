# Privacy Policy — Spot The Difference: Find It
**Effective Date:** September 2026

## 1. Introduction
Welcome to **Spot The Difference: Find It**. Your privacy is very important to us. This Privacy Policy outlines how our game operates and confirms that we do not collect personal identifying information.

## 2. Information Collection & Local Storage
- **No Personal Data Collected:** We do not collect, request, or transmit your name, email address, phone number, physical address, contacts, or photos.
- **Local Game Progress:** All game state — including unlocked levels, cleared levels, coins balance, hint inventory, and audio preferences — is stored purely locally on your device using AsyncStorage.
- **Data Reset / Deletion:** You can erase all saved gameplay data at any time via the "Reset All Progress" button in Settings.

## 3. Advertisements & Google AdMob
Our game displays advertisements provided by Google AdMob (such as banner ads, interstitials, and optional rewarded video ads). Google AdMob may collect standard device identifiers (such as the Google Advertising ID) in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy.
Visit: [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use their services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

## 4. Children's Privacy (COPPA & Family Policy)
Spot The Difference is designed as a family-friendly puzzle game suitable for players of all ages. We do not knowingly collect any personally identifiable information from children under 13.

## 5. Contact Us
If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please reach out via our Google Play Store developer listing.
