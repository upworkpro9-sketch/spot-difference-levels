Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 2026 | Spot The Difference: Find It

1. Introduction

Welcome to Spot The Difference: Find It. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines our data practices and confirms that your gaming experience is private and secure.

2. Information Collection & Storage

We do NOT collect any personally identifiable information (such as your name, email, phone number, physical address, contacts, or photos).

  • Local Progress: All game data — including unlocked levels, cleared levels, virtual coins, and preferences — is saved exclusively on your local device.
  • Data Control: You can completely erase all stored game data at any time via the "Reset All Progress" option in the in-game Settings.

3. Third-Party Services & Advertisements

Our game displays advertisements provided by Google AdMob (including banner, interstitial, and optional rewarded video ads). Google AdMob may collect standard device identifiers (such as the Google Advertising ID) and general diagnostic data to deliver non-personalized or personalized ads in accordance with Google's Advertising Policies.

For more information, please visit How Google uses information from sites or apps that use their services.

4. Children's Privacy (COPPA & Family Policy)

Spot The Difference is designed as a family-friendly game suitable for all ages. We comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Google Play's Families Policy. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13.

5. Network Usage

The game requires internet connectivity solely to download new free puzzle images from our content servers and load advertisements. No telemetry or personal information is transmitted to our servers.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or feedback regarding this Privacy Policy, please reach out through our developer profile on the Google Play Store.
